# Glossary

Words used across the apps and this wiki, in alphabetical order.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept** | A bartender claims a pending order; from then on only they can start, complete or decline it. |
| **Active** | The order is being prepared (the bartender pressed Start). Guests see "Being prepared". |
| **Area** | A part of the bar on the floor plan, such as "Inside" or "Terrace", with its own grid of cells. |
| **Average cost** | A stock item's pack cost after deliveries: the old and the new stock averaged by quantity. |
| **Base unit** | What a stock item is counted in: millilitres, grams or pieces. Every quantity is stored in it. |
| **Business day** | The day the bar works, starting at 06:00 (by default) rather than midnight, so a night is one day. "Today" on the dashboard, My shift and the register means the business day. |
| **Call bartender** | A guest's request from the customer app for a bartender to come to the table. |
| **Cancel** | A guest takes back their order while it is still pending. |
| **Cost of goods** | What the ingredients of the served orders cost, at the cost they had when they left the stock. |
| **Count** | A stock count: the counted quantities of chosen groups are entered and the differences are applied to the stock when it is closed. |
| **Counter order** | An order without a table, taken at the bar counter, optionally with a name to call. |
| **Decline** | Refusing an order, always with a reason the guest sees. A bartender can decline a pending order or their own accepted one; the admin can decline any open order. |
| **Delivery** | Goods received from a supplier, recorded in packs with their price; it adds stock and updates the average cost. |
| **Fixture** | A non-table piece on the floor plan: bar counter, entrance or wall. |
| **Gross profit** | Revenue minus cost of goods. |
| **Level** | A stock item's state: Ok, Low (at or below par) or Out (nothing left). |
| **Movement** | One change to a stock item's quantity: opening, waste, correction, sale, delivery or count. Never edited. |
| **Open order** | An order that is pending, accepted or active. |
| **Pack** | How a stock item is bought: a bottle, keg or case, holding a number of base units. Costs are entered per pack. |
| **Par level** | The quantity at or below which a stock item counts as low and is suggested for reordering. |
| **Pending** | A new order waiting in the queue for a bartender. Guests see "Sent to the bar". |
| **Recipe** | What one serving of a menu item takes from the stock, e.g. 40 ml gin and 100 ml tonic. |
| **Refund due** | Money the register took that has to be handed back because the order was declined or cancelled. |
| **Release** | Putting an open order back in the queue, which happens when its bartender is deactivated. |
| **Served** | The guest's word for a completed order. |
| **Serving** | One portion of a menu item, as its recipe describes. |
| **Sold out** | An item one of whose ingredients has less than one serving left; guests can't order it until stock comes back. |
| **Source** | Where an order came from: a guest (customer app), a bartender (Bartender app) or the register. |
| **Table code** | A table's short name, such as T7, printed in its QR code and used when ordering. |
| **To pay** | An unpaid order that still has to be paid at the register. |
| **Withdraw** | A bartender takes back an order they entered while it is still pending. |
